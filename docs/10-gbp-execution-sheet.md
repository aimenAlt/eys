## ONGOING SYSTEMS — build these once

### The missed-call flow
Google's LSA documentation says plainly: *"missed calls may negatively affect your responsiveness"* — and responsiveness is a named ad-rank input. Four of the five providers above you carry a reply-time badge. You carry none. This fixes a lead leak and a ranking factor with one build.

1. Fire the text in **under 60 seconds**. Value decays fast.
2. Lead with a human sentence, not a bare link:
   > *Sorry we missed you — this is EYS in Katy. What are you working on? Send a couple of photos here and we'll get you a number: [link]*
3. Ask for photos explicitly. It's what turns a vague call into a quotable job.
4. **After 8 PM, route differently:**
   > *We're still up — we can come out tonight at our after-hours rate, or book you first thing tomorrow. Which works?*

That last one is a product you currently sell nowhere.

### The review engine — 6–10 per month, forever
Recency beats volume. Every owner response on your profile is dated "a month ago," which means there's no system, only catch-up.

1. Ask in person at the walkthrough, with the finished work in the room.
2. Jobber automation: **one SMS, two hours after job completion**, with the direct Google review link. SMS, not email.
3. The ask, verbatim: *"If you have a minute, it really helps if you say what we did and how it went — in your own words."*
4. One follow-up after five days. Then stop.
5. Respond to every review inside 48 hours.

**Never:** incentives, gating, scripted content, asking for a star rating, asking them to mention a name or a city, or filtering by how happy someone seemed. And no bursts — spiky velocity on a twice-suspended profile is exactly what you don't want to look like.

### Monthly export — the 6th of every month
GBP holds **six months** of Performance data then deletes it, and suppresses search terms under 15 impressions. You've already lost visibility twice; the export is the only record recovery will ever have. Two minutes.
