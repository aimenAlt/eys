Builder-basic wire shelving works fine for a season, but most closets outgrow it fast. Elevate Your Space installs reach-in closet systems, pantry and linen shelving, and mudroom organization across Katy and West Houston — wire, laminate, or wood, sized to the actual space instead of forced to fit a stock kit.

## Who this service is for

- New-build buyers whose closets shipped with a single wire shelf and rod
- Families outgrowing a closet's current setup as kids grow or storage needs change
- Homeowners staging a resale home who want closets to show well
- Anyone who bought a kit (Elfa, ClosetMaid, or similar) and wants it installed correctly

## What we install

Wire and wood reach-in systems, pantry and linen-closet shelving, and mudroom or entry organization built around how you actually store things. We can install a kit you've already purchased, or plan a custom-fit layout when a closet has odd angles, a low header, or storage needs a boxed kit won't solve. Everything is anchored into studs wherever possible, with rated hardware sized to the material and expected load — not drywall alone holding up a loaded shelf.
