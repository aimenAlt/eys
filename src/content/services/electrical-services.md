Most calls we receive are about fixtures and fans — not full-panel rewires. Elevate Your Space handles clearly scoped ceiling-fan and light-fixture installation across Katy and West Houston when existing conditions support that work. Where a project requires licensed electrical contracting, permits, new circuits, or other regulated scope, that work must be identified and handled by an appropriately qualified professional.

## Who this is for

- Homeowners replacing dated lighting in Katy-area homes
- New-build buyers installing fixtures and fans after closing
- Anyone hanging heavier chandeliers or outdoor-rated fixtures who wants careful mounting
- Homeowners who need a clear answer on what fits handyman fixture scope versus licensed electrical work

## What we commonly do

Light fixture swaps and [ceiling fan installation](/services/ceiling-fan-installation/) on confirmed existing boxes. We assess mounting conditions before we start. New circuits, service upgrades, and other regulated electrical contracting are outside what this page offers — those needs should be handled by an appropriately licensed professional.

On approved installs we shut power down at the correct breaker, verify the circuit is dead, install fixtures level and secure, and restore covers cleanly. High ceilings and foyer installs may need extra staging; we plan access so the finished chandelier or pendant hangs correctly.
