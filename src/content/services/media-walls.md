A media wall is one of the highest-impact upgrades in a West Houston living room — the place where TV, lighting, storage, and architecture meet. Elevate Your Space designs and builds custom media walls in Katy, Cypress, Fulshear, Richmond, and nearby communities, with the same finish standard we bring to [custom carpentry](/services/custom-carpentry/) and secure [TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/).

## Who this is for

- Homeowners who want a finished entertainment wall, not just a bracket on drywall
- New builds and move-ins that need a focal wall before furniture lands
- Remodels pairing a fireplace, niches, or wood-slat accents with a clean TV center
- Families who want cable paths, lighting, and proportions planned before holes go in the wall

## What a media wall project can include

Built-in niche walls, wood-slat feature walls, marble-look or tile centers, floating consoles, LED niche lighting, and fireplace TV stacks when the equipment and clearances allow. Scope is custom: some projects are a refined slat wall and mount; others are full floor-to-ceiling built-ins. We say clearly what fits a handyman-led build versus what needs a trade partner.

Related work often pairs with [TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/), [custom carpentry](/services/custom-carpentry/), and [curtain installation](/services/curtain-installation/) when the whole room is being finished together.
