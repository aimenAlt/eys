Kitchen upgrades at Elevate Your Space mean high-impact improvements — hardware, cabinet adjustments, finishing details, and coordinated punch lists — not a full gut renovation. That is the gap many West Houston homeowners actually need filled: a kitchen that looks and works better without living through months of demolition.

## Who this is for

- Katy homeowners modernizing 1990s–2000s kitchens without a six-month renovation
- New-build owners upgrading builder-grade cabinets and lighting after closing
- Richmond and Fulshear families who want a kitchen that works harder for weeknight cooking
- Sellers who need a kitchen refresh that photographs well and still feels practical day to day

## What we typically deliver

[Cabinet installation](/services/cabinet-installation/) and hardware upgrades, select cabinet adjustments, fixture coordination, minor carpentry, backsplash and finish sequencing, and the trim details that make a kitchen feel intentional. When plumbing, gas, or major electrical changes are required, the scope must identify the appropriate qualified professional — EYS does not perform regulated trade work.

Projects often start with photos and a priority list — island refresh, hardware, lighting, or a more complete cabinet and finish package. You receive a free estimate with sequence and scope notes so you know what happens in which phase.
