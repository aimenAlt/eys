Tile is unforgiving work. Lippage between tiles, grout lines that wander, a cut edge left raw at a transition — every mistake stays visible for as long as the tile is on the wall. Elevate Your Space installs finish tile across Katy and West Houston: backsplashes, shower and tub surrounds, floor tile, and accent walls.

## Who this service is for

- Homeowners replacing a dated or builder-basic kitchen backsplash
- Anyone whose shower or tub surround needs retiling rather than another patch
- New-build buyers adding a feature or accent tile wall the builder did not offer
- Homeowners whose grout and caulk have failed and want the tile itself kept

## What we install

Kitchen and bar backsplashes, shower and tub surrounds, floor tile in bathrooms, entries, laundry rooms and mudrooms, accent and feature walls, and grout and caulk renewal on tile that is otherwise sound.

Substrate is where tile jobs are won or lost. We prep the surface before anything is set, cut edges and transitions cleanly rather than hiding them under trim as an afterthought, and caulk changes of plane instead of grouting them — grout in a corner cracks, which is why so many showers fail at the same three lines.

**Scope, honestly:** this is finish tile work. Slab work, structural repair, and exterior paving are outside what we take on. If a shower is being rebuilt from the studs and needs waterproofing done to spec, that has to happen correctly before tile — and we will tell you that at estimate rather than tile over it.
