Most home projects that go badly do not fail on craftsmanship — they fail in the gaps between trades. The carpenter finishes and leaves. Three weeks later you find a drywall crew. They finish, and now you need an electrician who cannot come until the following week. Nobody owns the result, and every handoff is yours to manage.

Elevate Your Space runs multi-trade projects differently across Katy and West Houston: Eyad does the carpentry himself, brings in the other trades, and manages the schedule from the first walkthrough to the last.

## Who this service is for

- Homeowners whose project needs carpentry, drywall, and electrical rather than just one of them
- Anyone who has already tried coordinating separate trades and does not want to do it again
- Buyers finishing a new build who want the whole room done, not one feature at a time
- Homeowners who want a single written number for the project before it starts

## How a multi-trade project runs

We start with a walkthrough of the actual room. You get **one written estimate** covering the full project — every trade, not just our portion — so there are no separate quotes to chase or reconcile.

From there, sequencing is the job. Carpentry, drywall, and electrical have to happen in a specific order, and each trade has to leave the space ready for the next. That coordination is what we take on. **Licensed electricians are brought in for regulated electrical work**, and our trade partners are licensed and insured for the work they perform. Elevate Your Space Handyman is not advertising as a licensed electrician or electrical contractor — we manage the schedule, we do not perform that work ourselves.

You talk to one person throughout, and the same person walks the finished project with you at the end.

## A worked example: the media wall

A built-in [media wall](/services/media-walls/) is the clearest illustration, because it genuinely needs three trades:

- **Carpentry** — the built-in structure, niches, floating shelves, and finish work, done in-house
- **Drywall** — hanging and finishing around the new structure, with texture matched to the existing wall so the addition does not read as an add-on panel
- **Licensed electrical** — power relocated behind the display and low-voltage routed for concealed cabling, handled by a licensed electrician

Run as three separate hires, that is three schedules, three estimates, and two handoffs where responsibility gets blurry. Run as one project, it is one number and one walkthrough.
