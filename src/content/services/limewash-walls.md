Limewash has become a popular alternative to standard paint for homeowners who want a softer, more textured, more organic-looking wall than a flat acrylic finish can give. Elevate Your Space applies limewash accent and feature walls across Katy and West Houston, with the same surface prep discipline we use for wallpaper and painted accent walls.

## Who this service is for

- Homeowners wanting a soft, matte, textured accent or feature wall instead of flat paint
- Anyone who likes the limewash look from design inspiration photos and wants it done correctly on a real wall
- Homeowners replacing a builder-grade textured wall with a finished, elevated look
- New-build homeowners finishing a room with a feature-wall moment

## What goes into a limewash wall

Limewash is a mineral-based finish, not standard paint, and it behaves differently — coverage, drying, and coat count all depend on the wall's existing texture and the look you want. Most Katy-area homes have textured builder walls that benefit from being floated smoother first, the same prep wallpaper needs, so the limewash texture reads intentionally rather than uneven. We scope surface prep, coat count, and pigment as part of the estimate rather than treating every wall the same.
