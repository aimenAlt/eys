## Handyman and home upgrades across Richmond, Fort Bend County

Richmond is not one housing market — it is several living side by side. Historic downtown properties, established estates in **Pecan Grove**, and a wave of master-planned communities each demand a different approach to home improvement. Elevate Your Space Handyman (EYS) serves Fort Bend homeowners across that spectrum, with concentrated work in three distinctive communities: **Aliana**, **Harvest Green**, and **Veranda**.

Each neighborhood has its own identity, architectural character, and typical project list. EYS adjusts scope and materials accordingly — whether you need [drywall repair](/services/drywall-repair/) in a 1990s subdivision or a custom shiplap wall in a modern farmhouse build.

### Aliana: resort amenities and everyday upgrades

**Aliana** is one of Richmond’s most sought-after master-planned communities in the 77407 zip code, built around resort-style clubhouses — **The Club at Aliana** and **The Westmoor Club** — plus extensive trail systems and a strong community spirit. Homes here were primarily constructed over the last decade by builders like Perry Homes and Highland Homes, which means properties are modern but often ready for personalized upgrades.

Common EYS projects in Aliana include:

- **Garage overhead storage** — heavy-duty racks for seasonal items, bikes, and gear, reclaiming floor space in Texas heat
- **[TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/)** in living rooms and game rooms with clean wire management
- **Patio furniture assembly** for outdoor entertaining before neighborhood gatherings
- **Cabinet hardware updates** on kitchen and bath cabinetry
- **Bundled [handyman to-do list](/services/handyman-to-do-list/) visits** for accumulated maintenance items
- **Outdoor patio improvements** — fans, mounting, and covered-space finishing for warm Richmond evenings

The **Lagos at Aliana** 55+ neighborhood draws active adults who want home projects handled with minimal disruption — organized visits focused on fixture swaps, shelving, hardware refreshes, and garage layouts designed for low-maintenance living.

Aliana’s lifestyle centers on pools, tennis courts, and greenbelt walks — not Saturdays deciphering furniture assembly instructions. Send your address and project list for confirmation, and EYS scopes the work around your schedule.

### Harvest Green: Houston’s first agri-hood

**Harvest Green** offers a lifestyle unlike anywhere else in Richmond — a 12-acre working **Village Farm**, the **Farmhouse** amenity center, weekly farmers markets, and **Farm Club** programming rooted in sustainable, outdoor-focused living. Johnson Development built the community around a modern farmhouse and contemporary aesthetic that homeowners often want to extend inside their own walls.

EYS projects in Harvest Green frequently align with that character:

- **[Custom carpentry](/services/custom-carpentry/)** — shiplap accent walls, board-and-batten, and mudroom benches by the garage entry for dirty shoes and garden tools
- **Garage storage** for landscaping equipment, outdoor gear, and supplies from Village Farm activities
- **Modern farmhouse lighting** — swapping builder-grade fixtures for statement pendants and ceiling fans
- **[Curtain installation](/services/curtain-installation/)** with properly leveled rods and heavy drapery
- **Patio and outdoor living upgrades** for relaxing after a day at the Farm Club
- **Media walls and floating shelves** in rustic wood tones that complement the neighborhood aesthetic

Life in Harvest Green means getting hands dirty at the farm, picking up produce at the market, or relaxing by the pools at The Farmhouse. EYS handles the measuring, carpentry, and installation so your time at home supports that lifestyle rather than competing with it.

### Veranda: front-porch Southern charm

**Veranda** is designed around connection, historical Southern charm, and the iconic front porch — a social architecture that shapes how residents live and entertain. The community scene centers on **The Cottage House**, event lawns, and landmarks like the giant rocking chair that define Veranda’s welcoming identity.

Projects in Veranda reflect porch-centric living and community pride:

- **Outdoor-rated ceiling fans on front porches** — among the most requested services for comfortable Fort Bend evenings
- **Patio furniture assembly** — rocking chairs, sectionals, and dining sets for porch and backyard spaces
- **Patio TV mounting** for outdoor movie nights with neighbors
- **Trim work and wainscoting** that matches Veranda’s traditional architectural details
- **Custom drop-zones and built-ins** for busy families coming through the front door
- **Interior [TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/)**, shelving, and hardware updates throughout the home

Veranda residents take pride in how their homes look from the street — porch fans, clean trim, and assembled outdoor furniture matter as much as interior finishes. EYS approaches these projects with floor protection, dust control during carpentry, and cleanup that respects a tight-knit neighborhood where reputation travels quickly.

When porch or exterior work may be visible from the street, customers should confirm current association requirements before scheduling. EYS executes approved projects to specification.

### Established Richmond homes

Beyond the master-planned communities, Richmond includes historic areas and established subdivisions like **Pecan Grove** where homeowners request modernization rather than move-in finishing: trim and hardware updates for 1980s–1990s interiors, [drywall repair](/services/drywall-repair/), door alignment, paint refresh, and general maintenance that protects long-term value.

The mindset shifts from customization to careful restoration — but the standard remains the same: honest scoping, skilled execution, and a clean finish.
