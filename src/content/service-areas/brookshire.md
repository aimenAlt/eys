## Handyman services in Brookshire, west of Katy on I-10

Brookshire is a Waller County city of about 5,066 residents (2020 census), sitting on Interstate 10 roughly seven miles west of Katy and 34 miles west of downtown Houston. Named for Nathan Brookshire, a captain in the Texian Army, the town filed its first plats in 1893 and incorporated in 1946. Today the 77423 ZIP code mixes that older town with fast-growing subdivisions, and Elevate Your Space Handyman (EYS) serves both.
