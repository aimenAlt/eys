## Handyman services in Fulshear’s growing master-planned communities

Fulshear has transformed from a small Fort Bend crossroads into one of the region's most sought-after addresses for new construction and nature-forward living — U.S. Census Bureau estimates named it the #2 fastest-growing U.S. city with 20,000-plus residents for a third consecutive year, growing roughly 21% in a single recent year to around 64,630 residents. Elevate Your Space Handyman (EYS) serves Fulshear homeowners at the moment builders hand over the keys — and in the months afterward, when a house still needs the finishing touches that make it feel like home.

Two communities define much of our Fulshear work: **Cross Creek Ranch**, celebrated as a “Naturehood” woven into native landscape and trail systems, and **Jordan Ranch**, where agri-hood amenities and resort-style recreation anchor a rapidly expanding Johnson Development community.

### Cross Creek Ranch: trails, stewardship, and organized living

**Cross Creek Ranch** integrates homes with restored **Flewellen Creek**, native landscaping, and more than 60 miles of winding trails. The community draws active families, nature lovers, and a significant 55+ population in the gated **Bonterra** neighborhood — each with different home improvement priorities united by a respect for quality construction.

Typical EYS projects in Cross Creek Ranch include:

- **Garage overhead storage** — heavy-duty racks for bikes, kayaks, and hiking gear used on community trails and lakes, reclaiming floor space for vehicles
- **[Custom carpentry](/services/custom-carpentry/)** — mudroom benches, crown molding, wainscoting, and drop-zones for gear coming off the trail
- **[TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/)** in main living rooms and covered patios
- **Outdoor-rated ceiling fans** on high covered patios to combat Texas summers
- **Trim work and accent walls** — shiplap or wood-slat focal points for modern transitional and Texas traditional floor plans
- **Move-in punch lists** for newly constructed homes still settling into daily routines

Life in Cross Creek Ranch revolves around **Adventure Island**, the 18-hole disc golf course, and casual dining at the Italian Maid Café — not a Saturday spent wrestling with furniture instructions. EYS handles assembly, mounting, and organization so weekends stay outdoors.

For **Bonterra** active-adult homeowners, projects tend toward respectful, scoped visits: fixture updates, shelving, hardware refreshes, and garage layouts designed for low-maintenance living — executed with floor protection and minimal disruption.

### Jordan Ranch: agri-hood living and new-build finishing

**Jordan Ranch** combines historic ranch heritage with modern resort amenities — **Sunset Farm** for farm-to-table programming, **The Shed** with its lazy river, and community events organized around an active social calendar. Because Jordan Ranch is still expanding rapidly, most residents are moving into brand-new construction from builders like Lennar and others across the development.

New Jordan Ranch homeowners typically need the essential post-closing upgrades builders do not include:

- **[TV mounting](/services/tv-mounting/)** on fresh drywall or masonry fireplaces
- **Ceiling fan installation** in bedrooms and living spaces with builder pre-wires
- **Cabinet hardware** on builder-grade kitchen and bath cabinetry — a high-impact, same-week customization
- **[Curtain installation](/services/curtain-installation/)** for privacy in newly constructed rooms
- **Garage overhead storage** for gardening tools, soil, and gear tied to Sunset Farm’s programming
- **Furniture assembly** — beds, dining sets, and patio furniture before the first neighborhood gathering
- **Bundled [handyman to-do list](/services/handyman-to-do-list/) visits** that knock out an entire move-in punch list in one appointment

The gap between receiving keys and actually parking in the garage is often a list of mounting, assembly, and organization tasks. EYS scopes that list from your photos, coordinates timing around your closing, and executes with floor protection suited to unblemished new finishes.

When a project requires licensed trade support — swapping a hardwired chandelier, adding outlets for a wall-mounted TV, or new exterior electrical for a patio fan — EYS can help coordinate appropriate specialists alongside installation work.

While much of Fulshear skews toward new construction, the city’s growth also brings maturing subdivisions that need [drywall repair](/services/drywall-repair/), paint touch-ups, door adjustments, and general maintenance.
