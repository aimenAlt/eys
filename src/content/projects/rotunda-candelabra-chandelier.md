Getting a fixture centered in a stepped, domed rotunda ceiling two stories up takes more than a ladder — this install went up on rolling scaffolding built in stages inside the stairwell, with the chandelier wired and hung once the platform reached ceiling height.

The finished candelabra chandelier now sits centered in the dome, lighting the curved staircase below. This falls under our [electrical services](/services/electrical-services/) for high-reach fixture installs. Browse more fixture and lighting work in our [gallery](/our-work/).
