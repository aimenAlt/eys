A plain wall becomes a finished feature with a full run of two-tier picture-frame wainscoting: a tall upper panel over a shorter lower panel, repeated across four bays and trimmed with matching crown and base. Outlets were worked around cleanly rather than boxed in.

This is the same picture-frame approach behind our other accent-wall work, here run across an entire wall rather than a single feature panel. See more [custom carpentry](/services/custom-carpentry/) and [trim work](/our-work/?category=carpentry-built-ins) in our gallery.
