This kitchen went from a dated layout to a two-tone finish: sage green on the lower cabinets and island, warm white on the uppers, tied together with gold pendant lighting, a gold pull-down faucet, and quartz counters. A white subway tile backsplash runs the full length of the range wall.

The island anchors the room with an open-shelf end that faces into the adjoining living and dining space, keeping the kitchen connected to the rest of the floor plan rather than closed off.
