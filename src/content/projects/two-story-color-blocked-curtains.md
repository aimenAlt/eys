These curtain panels use a color-blocked design — gold through the body of the panel, navy at the header and tieback band — which means keeping the color break level across every panel on a two-story rod run. Four panels now hang floor to ceiling across the window wall, alongside a round crystal pendant chandelier centered in the room.
