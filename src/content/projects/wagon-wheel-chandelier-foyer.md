A two-story foyer gets one shot at a first impression, and a fixture this size has to hang dead level from the moment you walk in. This sixteen-light wagon-wheel chandelier, with glass candle covers around a black iron ring, was hung on a matching chain and leveled from a ceiling mount well above reach.
