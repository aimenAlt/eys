A plain hallway wall becomes the focal point of the house with a full run of picture-frame molding, carried floor to ceiling and painted matte black against white baseboards. The doorway cut into the wall is trimmed to match the panel lines on either side, so the opening reads as part of the design instead of interrupting it.

This is the same picture-frame technique behind our other accent-wall work, just taken to a bolder, darker finish — proof it scales from a soft painted tone to a high-contrast statement wall depending on what the room calls for.
