A statement fixture like this lives or dies on how level and centered it hangs from two stories up — a single globe out of line is obvious from every angle in a stairwell this open. Each of the fourteen glass globes on this cascading chandelier was staggered to a planned height and leveled from the ceiling mount down, so the whole fixture reads as one deliberate composition rather than a tangle of cords. Wiring connections and the canopy cover finish the install so the ceiling stays neat above the stairwell.
