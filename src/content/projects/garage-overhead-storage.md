This garage needed real storage capacity without eating into parking space, so the build went up and across instead of onto the floor: a long custom wood shelf runs the width of the wall on triangular brackets, and a wire overhead rack is anchored into the ceiling joists above the garage door track.

Both are built to hold real weight — bins, seasonal gear, and anything else that doesn't need to live on the floor — with mounting points sized to the joists and studs rather than just the drywall. It's the same approach behind our [garage storage](/services/garage-storage-solutions/) work generally: shelving that's rated for what actually gets stored on it, not just what looks good empty.
