This bedroom wall runs a two-tier picture-frame molding pattern — a row of tall panels above a shorter wainscoting run below — carried the full length of the room. Every miter lines up and the paint finish matches the existing trim exactly, so the wall reads as part of the house's original millwork rather than an addition.

It's the same picture-frame approach we use for hallway and living-room accent walls, scaled to a bedroom and finished in a softer tone to sit quietly behind the furniture rather than compete with it.
