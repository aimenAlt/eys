This kitchen pairs a slate-blue island — quartz counters, black hardware, open shelving on one end — with white perimeter cabinetry that keeps the room feeling bright and open. A black multi-glass pendant hangs over the island as the room's one dark accent besides the island color itself.

The adjoining window nook picks up the same white cabinetry for a built-in bench seat and a glass-front hutch, so the kitchen and the sitting area read as one finished space rather than two separate rooms bolted together.
